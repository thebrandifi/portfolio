FAQ

Questions, answered.

What services do you offer?

Digital marketing strategy, Meta Ads and Google Ads management, social media management, content creation, email marketing, marketing analytics, AI video production and motion graphics. Pick one or combine them; the Services section above has the detail.

Where are you based, and do you work with clients outside Ghana?

I’m based in Accra, Ghana. Strategy, content and AI-powered video can all be delivered remotely, so brands outside Ghana are welcome to get in touch.

Are the videos in your work filmed or AI-made?

They are AI-assisted motion content, built with generative AI tools and Canva. They are not traditionally filmed productions, and I’ll always say so.

Can I see the full videos?

Yes. Most clips on this page are 10-second previews and full versions are available on request; the two sciatica awareness videos are shown in full. Message me on WhatsApp or by email.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope: platforms, how much content you need, video length and timelines. Tell me about your business and goals and I’ll respond with a clear proposal.

How do we get started?

Send a message through the form, WhatsApp or email. Tell me about your business, your audience and what you want to achieve, and we’ll take it from there.
