Privacy

Privacy.

This website is run by Appau Kofi Enoch in Accra, Ghana. It is built to collect as little information as possible.

The contact form

The form does not send your details to a server on this website. When you submit it, your message opens in WhatsApp so you can send it to me. You can also email me directly. Those services handle your message under their own policies.

What stays in your browser

The site remembers your bright or dark theme choice and, if you click “Enquire about this”, the service you picked. This is stored only in your own browser and is not sent anywhere.

Third-party services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when a page loads. Links to Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy practices. Share buttons only open the service’s own share page.

Analytics and cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. If analytics such as Google Analytics are added later, this page will be updated to say so.

If you contact me

If you message or email me, I use your details only to reply and to discuss the work you asked about. You can ask me at any time to delete a conversation or the details you shared.
