About Appau Kofi Enoch

Appau Kofi Enoch: the mind behind the strategy.

I turn customer conversations into content that sells.

I learned selling first. Since 2015 I’ve sold face to face, led teams and built my own business, HLB Plux Enterprise, so I know what makes a customer say yes. Now I bring that into digital marketing: social media content, product promotions and campaigns shaped by what customers ask, what the market is doing and what competitors are up to.

I use Canva with ChatGPT, Claude and Google Flow to take an idea from script to finished visual, quickly. As a Digital Marketing with AI student of IPMC University College, I’m open to brands and teams who want content that starts real conversations.
