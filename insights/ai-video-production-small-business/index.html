How AI video production works for small businesses

By Appau Kofi Enoch · 24 November 2026 · 5 min read

AI-assisted video lets small businesses make promotional videos and explainers faster than traditional production. It is a production method with real strengths and real limits, so it helps to know how it works before you commission one.

What “AI video production” means

Generative AI tools create images, video clips and voices from written instructions. A person still directs the work: the message, the script, the structure, the brand look and the final edit. The result is AI-assisted content. It is not filmed footage, and it should be described honestly as that.

The process, step by step

  1. Brief: the goal, the audience, the offer and where the video will run.
  2. Script and storyboard: a strong first few seconds, one clear message and a call to action.
  3. Visuals: AI-generated images and clips, combined with your logo, colours and product images.
  4. Voiceover and music: an AI voice or a recorded one, with the rights checked.
  5. Edit: pacing, captions and sound, usually in vertical 9:16 for social platforms.
  6. Review: check facts, brand details and claims, then revise.
  7. Export: the right sizes for each platform.

Where it works well

  • Product promotions and advertisements.
  • Explainers and educational videos.
  • Recruitment and announcement videos.
  • Short vertical videos for social media and ads.

You can see examples of health education, retail and recruitment videos on my Works page.

Where to be careful

  • Accuracy: anything about health, money or safety should be checked by a qualified person before it is published.
  • Realism: do not present AI-generated people as real customers or real staff.
  • Brand details: logos, product shapes and text need checking, because AI tools can get them wrong.
  • Rights: make sure you can use any voice, music or image in the video.

What to prepare

  • Your logo files and brand colours.
  • Product photos or descriptions.
  • The main message and the offer.
  • The action you want viewers to take.
  • Where the video will run, and any videos you like for reference.

Cost and timing

These depend on the length, the number of versions and how much revision is needed, so it is best to share the brief and ask for a quote rather than rely on a standard price.
