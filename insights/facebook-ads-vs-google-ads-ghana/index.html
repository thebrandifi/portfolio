Facebook Ads vs Google Ads: which is better for businesses in Ghana?

By Appau Kofi Enoch · 24 November 2026 · 5 min read

Both Facebook (Meta) ads and Google Ads can bring customers to a business in Ghana. They work in very different ways, so the better choice depends on what you sell and who you want to reach.

The short answer

Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for something. Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) puts your offer in front of people who fit your audience while they scroll. One catches existing demand; the other helps create it.

How Google Ads works

Search ads appear when someone types a query such as “electrician in Accra”. You choose the search terms you want to appear for, write the ads and send people to a page or a contact option. Usually you pay when someone clicks.

  • Best for: services and products people already look for, and urgent needs.
  • Watch out for: it needs people to be searching, and popular search terms can be expensive.

How Facebook and Instagram ads work

Meta Ads Manager shows your ads in feeds, Stories and Reels. You target by location, interests and behaviour, or by people who already know your business. Ads can send people to a website, to Messenger or to a WhatsApp conversation.

  • Best for: visual products, new offers, brand awareness and businesses that sell through conversations.
  • Watch out for: people were not searching, so the creative has to earn attention in a second or two.

How to choose

  • People already search for what you sell: start with Google Ads.
  • Your product is new or people do not know to look for it: start with Meta Ads.
  • You sell something visual, such as fashion, food or beauty: Meta Ads usually shows it best.
  • You want customers to message you on WhatsApp: use Meta ads that open a WhatsApp chat.
  • You have the budget for both: use Meta to build interest and Google to capture the people who then search.

Starting with a small budget

  1. Pick one goal, for example enquiries or WhatsApp messages.
  2. Pick one audience and one location.
  3. Make two or three versions of the ad and let them run for several days before judging.
  4. Change one thing at a time so you know what made the difference.
  5. Use a budget you can afford to learn from, and use Ads Manager rather than only boosting posts, because it gives you more control.

What to measure

Likes and clicks are not business results. Track enquiries, WhatsApp messages and sales, and work out what each enquiry costs. Google Analytics shows what visitors do on your website, and asking new customers how they heard about you fills in the rest.

Common mistakes

  • Running ads without a clear offer or a clear next step.
  • Sending people to a slow or confusing page.
  • Replying to enquiries too late, which wastes the ad spend.
  • Stopping after two days, or changing everything at once.
